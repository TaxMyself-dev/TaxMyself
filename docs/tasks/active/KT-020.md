## Business outcome

KeepInTax gains a centrally branded WhatsApp channel that can safely support
product-use questions, deliver generated documents, and receive customer
documents without moving business ownership away from the existing
notification and document-import boundaries.

## Scope and constraints

- In scope: the eight gated stages (0-7) above, each separately reviewed, tested,
  documented, committed locally, demonstrated, and approved before the next.
- Out of scope: production access/deployment, pushing any branch without an
  explicit primary-manager request, per-accountant WABAs, marketing campaigns,
  tax/accounting advice, schema work before approval, and bypassing existing
  document-import or notification ownership.
- Required reading: root `AGENTS.md`; `docs/coordination/WORKFLOW.md`,
  `AUTHORITY.md`, `QUALITY_GATES.md`, `PRODUCTION_POLICY.md`, and `LESSONS.md`;
  `docs/tasks/README.md`; `docs/plans/whatsapp-integration.md`; and nearest
  topic `AGENTS.md` files before each affected module is touched.
- Approval boundaries: explicit approval is required for every transition to
  the next stage, all schema/data changes, live webhooks/API calls, credentials,
  paid services, external contract changes, authentication/authorization or
  security-policy expansion, and any production action. Production remains
  prohibited.
- Security invariants: raw-body signature verification precedes parsing;
  E.164/`wa_id` identity must be verified; operational and marketing consent
  remain distinct; opt-out is centrally enforced; retries are idempotent; and
  secrets, temporary media URLs, document content, and sensitive payloads are
  excluded from logs and committed artifacts.
- Architecture invariants: WhatsApp is a channel. Inbound documents remain
  owned by `DocumentImportService`; outgoing messages remain owned by the
  Notification/Outbox layer.
