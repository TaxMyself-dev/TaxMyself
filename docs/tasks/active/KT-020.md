## Business outcome

The admin Content tab presents the approved KeepInTax self-employed guide as an editable, interactive HTML presentation, including the Income Tax learning sequence and a live annual-tax simulator.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The Content tab launches an RTL 16:9 guide with previous/next navigation, keyboard navigation, progress, and a working fullscreen control.
- [ ] Previous and next buttons contain text only, without arrow icons.
- [ ] The guide implements the approved sequence: friendly cover, four business definitions, three tax authorities, Income Tax obligations, exempt-versus-micro distinction, common micro-route blockers, salary-plus-business-profit explanation, and interactive tax simulation.
- [ ] The authorities slide makes Income Tax an interactive entry point to the implemented detail sequence and clearly marks the other authority chapters as future content.
- [ ] The simulator recalculates business profit, combined taxable income, estimated 2026 income tax, credit-point reduction, salary withholding, and estimated balance as the inputs change.
- [ ] Year-specific thresholds, tax brackets, and credit-point value live in the central figures file rather than in presentation markup.
- [ ] Content remains editable HTML and accessible on smaller screens; approved illustrations are decorative and do not contain authoritative copy or figures.
- [ ] Focused frontend specs and the Angular production build pass, subject only to documented pre-existing test-runner failures.
- [ ] Admin-panel topic documentation reflects the completed guide and maintenance points.

## Scope and constraints

- In scope: admin content hub metadata, self-employed guide content/layouts/interaction, simulator logic, decorative assets, focused specs, and admin-panel topic documentation.
- Out of scope: backend, database/schema, production data, customer data, publishing/deployment, VAT or National Insurance detail chapters, and product pricing.
- Required reading: root `AGENTS.md`, `docs/coordination/WORKFLOW.md`, `AUTHORITY.md`, `QUALITY_GATES.md`, `LESSONS.md`, and `frontend/src/app/pages/admin-panel/AGENTS.md`.
- Approval boundaries: no schema, accounting/report totals, authorization, external API, production, or customer-data changes.
