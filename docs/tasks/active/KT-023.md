## Business outcome

Keepintax has a maintainable, evidence-based map of the Israeli Tax Authority
legal and technical requirements that may apply to its document-issuance and
bookkeeping capabilities, with official sources, applicability boundaries,
current-system evidence, prioritized gaps, and a practical verification path.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] `docs/compliance/tax-authority/README.md` maps the covered domains,
  authority hierarchy, evidence labels, limitations, and maintenance process.
- [x] `sources/` contains only useful official source documents that may be
  retained, without modifying them, plus metadata-only records where retention
  is unsuitable.
- [x] `source-index.md` records each source's official title, publisher, URL,
  publication/version/effective date where available, access date, retained
  filename or metadata-only reason, checksum, and short relevance note.
- [x] `requirements-matrix.md` distinguishes legal duties, technical
  instructions, recommendations, interpretations, and open questions and maps
  every material requirement to an exact source section/page and Keepintax
  implementation evidence or an explicit `not assessed` state.
- [x] The matrix covers document types and issuance, computerized bookkeeping,
  internal/external documentation, numbering/corrections/cancellations/credits,
  retention/backup/auditability, Israel Invoices/SHAAM allocation numbers,
  uniform-file specifications, computerized documents/e-signatures/delivery,
  VAT reports/interfaces, security/access/audit trail/retention, and known
  future effective dates.
- [x] `gaps-and-roadmap.md` prioritizes evidence-backed gaps without changing
  code or making unapproved legal/product decisions.
- [x] `testing-and-certification.md` defines automated and manual verification,
  samples, isolated test environments, and any official approval/certification
  authority that the sources actually identify.
- [x] Existing SHAAM research is explicitly reconciled into still-valid,
  superseded, and re-verification-required findings.
- [x] Every material legal ambiguity is labelled as requiring validation by an
  accountant, tax adviser, or lawyer; no interpretation is stated as law.
- [x] Documentation-only quality gates pass and a local documentation commit is
  produced for manager review.

## Scope and constraints

- In scope: repository/documentation audit; official primary-source research;
  official-source downloads under `docs/compliance/tax-authority/sources/`;
  requirements, gap, and testing/certification documentation; this task file.
- Out of scope: application code, schemas, SQL, configuration, infrastructure,
  secrets, production or production-copy access, live Tax Authority requests,
  real taxpayer/business identifiers, deployment, push, merge, or legal advice.
- Required reading: root `AGENTS.md`; `docs/coordination/WORKFLOW.md`,
  `AUTHORITY.md`, `QUALITY_GATES.md`, `PRODUCTION_POLICY.md`, and `LESSONS.md`;
  `backend/src/shaam/AGENTS.md`; existing SHAAM and related reporting/document
  research and topic documentation.
- Source restriction: primary official sources only (`gov.il`, Israel Tax
  Authority, official legislation/Reshumot/Nevo publication where authoritative,
  and official Tax Authority technical specifications).
- Approval boundaries: record implementation needs as gaps only. Stop on any
  request for production access, credentials, live external calls, schema/data
  changes, or a materially new product/legal decision.
