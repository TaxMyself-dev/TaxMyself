## Business outcome

A client represented by an active accountant may submit expenses and supporting
documents, but only the accountant may approve, edit, reclassify, or delete the
expense. Pending expenses do not affect journals, VAT, P&L, or depreciation.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] A represented client's manual expense is saved as pending and appears in
      the archive without creating a journal entry or depreciation posting.
- [x] A represented client is denied expense/document approval, accounting
      edits, deletion, and bank-transaction confirmation by the backend.
- [x] An authorized accountant can edit and atomically approve a pending manual
      expense from the archive.
- [x] Approval records the real accountant actor and creates exactly one journal
      entry plus applicable depreciation postings.
- [x] Pending equipment is excluded defensively from Form 1342.
- [x] Frontend actions reflect the backend policy without relying on the UI for
      security.
- [x] Unrepresented and revoked-delegation client behavior remains self-service.
- [x] Focused tests and backend/frontend builds pass.

## Scope and constraints

- In scope: expense/document approval authorization, manual expense submission,
  archive approval/edit UI, bank-confirmation authorization, depreciation report
  filtering, focused tests and topic documentation.
- Out of scope: database schema changes, approval history tables, daily accounting
  audit job, production data changes, deployment.
- Required reading: root and topic `AGENTS.md`, coordination workflow, categories
  redesign master plan.
- Approval boundaries: no schema or production changes; no expansion of tax or
  recognition rules beyond keeping pending rows out of accounting outputs.
