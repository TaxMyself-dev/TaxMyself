## Locked visible decisions

- The transition follows the recap and introduces practical daily conduct and
  realizing self-employed rights, with Kipi carrying a backpack.
- The combined slide shows the exempt path at the top: transaction account
  before payment, then receipt upon payment.
- The main authorized path shows transaction account before payment, then tax
  invoice-receipt upon payment. It is a cash-basis example for relevant liberal
  professions, not a universal timing rule for every authorized dealer.
- The framed authorized path shows tax invoice followed by receipt. It has no
  separate payment-timing caption.
- The artwork deliberately contains no percentages, no “or separately” label,
  and no explanatory captions beneath the document cards. Presenter context
  supplies qualifications and exceptions that are intentionally not added to
  the slide.

## Scope

This change is visual and navigational only. It does not change invoicing
logic, tax calculations, VAT reporting, backend behavior, or customer data.
