## Presenter qualifications retained outside slide

Assumes full eligible business use from the start of the year, regular actual
expense deduction (not an additional deduction atop the micro-business 30%),
and cost net of deductible VAT. These are expense amounts, not tax savings.
First/last-year depreciation depends on the actual usage period and available
remaining depreciable cost. Current expenses must qualify for deduction in the
relevant tax year.

The VAT slogan concerns eligible input VAT for an authorized dealer with the
required documentation and other deduction conditions. It is deducted through
the periodic VAT report, not spread over depreciation years; it is not an
unconditional immediate cash refund, and exempt dealers cannot deduct input VAT.
