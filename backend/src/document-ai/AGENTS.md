# Shared document AI queue

All document AI admission belongs here. One tenant may use all spare capacity; fairness applies only when tenants compete. Provider/model quotas and leases are shared through short DB transactions, never connection-holding locks during AI. Unknown paid outcomes must not be replayed or switched to another provider automatically. Preserve purpose-specific retention and ownership. No live DB, app bootstrap, schema application, existing-work migration, real providers or deletion during development.

Scheduler mode uses the OIDC-guarded request-bound round documented in ../../../docs/features/document-ai-scheduler.md. Keep the hard global ceiling at two, including completion; never return while a round job/heartbeat is pending. Preserve the shared admission transaction and do not reclaim a still-leased saved result. Promise.race may wait for a freed processing slot; it must never replace final cancellation/drain. Scheduler mode suppresses document cron wrappers and provides separate authenticated upload/cleanup maintenance. It does not migrate Gmail, protected PDF or classification workers. Processing refills at most two slots for 60 seconds; maintenance starts items for at most 60 seconds (ten uploads/ten cleanup candidates), and both endpoints drain before responding.
